# Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability

Please do not report security vulnerabilities through public GitHub issues, discussions, or pull requests.

Please send an email to `info@hololinked.dev`. We currently do not have a dedicated email address, but this one is closely monitored.

Discord messages sent to the maintainer's private account (please see the README) are also accepted.

When reporting a vulnerability, please include:

- The affected version (tag or commit SHA)
- A description of the issue and why you believe it is security-sensitive
- Steps to reproduce the issue or a proof of concept
- Any relevant logs, payloads, or screenshots
- The potential impact
- Any suggested mitigations or fixes, if known

Response times may vary, but we are usually able to respond more quickly on weekends than on weekdays.

We will do our best to address the issue as soon as possible.
